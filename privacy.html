Privacy Policy

Cloud Infrastructure is a private, non-commercial tool used by a small group of students.

What it accesses

With your permission it uses the Google Drive "drive.file" access, which lets it read and write only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

What it stores

An access token, kept in an encrypted secret store. No personal data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.

Your control

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or delete the "Cloud Infrastructure" folder in your Drive.
